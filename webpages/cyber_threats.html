Explore Gyanpuja Boarding School 🏫

Every Friday Programs and Their Advantages 🎉

At Gyanpuja Boarding School, we organize special programs every Friday to enhance students' skills and creativity. These programs include:

  • Karate Classes: Builds physical fitness, discipline, and self-defense skills.
  • Music Classes: Enhances creativity, improves concentration, and boosts confidence.
  • Dance Classes: Promotes physical health, teamwork, and self-expression.

These programs help students develop a well-rounded personality and discover their hidden talents.

Extra Curriculum Activities (ECAs) 🌟

We offer a variety of ECAs to help students explore their interests and improve their skills:

  • Karate Classes: Improves physical fitness, discipline, and self-defense skills.
  • Music Classes: Enhances creativity, concentration, and confidence.
  • Dance Classes: Promotes physical health, teamwork, and self-expression.

Additionally, we organize exciting contests to encourage healthy competition and talent development:

  • Quiz Contests: Tests general knowledge and critical thinking skills.
  • Dance Contests: Showcases creativity and rhythm.
  • Spelling Contests: Improves vocabulary and language skills.
  • Got Talent: A platform for students to showcase their unique talents.
  • Singing Contests: Encourages musical talent and confidence.

These activities and contests help students develop new skills, build confidence, and have fun!

Evaluation and Tests 📝

We conduct regular evaluations to ensure students' academic progress and overall development:

  • 4 Evaluation Exams: Comprehensive exams to assess students' understanding of the curriculum.
  • 4 Monthly Tests: Regular tests to track progress and identify areas for improvement.
  • Surprise Tests: Keeps students on their toes and encourages consistent preparation.

These evaluations help students stay focused, improve their performance, and prepare for future challenges.

Yearly Special Classes 🌟

Every year, we introduce new classes to broaden students' knowledge and skills:

  • Handwriting Classes: Improves writing skills and presentation.
  • Yoga Classes: Enhances physical and mental well-being.
  • Cyber Security Classes: Teaches students about online safety and digital responsibility.
  • Nepal Economics Classes: Provides insights into the country's economy and financial systems.
  • Animal Classes: Educates students about different types of animals and their importance in the ecosystem.

These classes aim to provide students with practical knowledge and skills that go beyond the traditional curriculum.
