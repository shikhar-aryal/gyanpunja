School Admission Process

At Gyanpuja Boarding School, we offer multiple admission processes to ensure every student has the opportunity to join our institution. Whether you're looking for a fresh start at the beginning of the year or need to transfer mid-term, we have a process tailored for you.

  • New Year Admission: Start the academic year with us! Admissions are open at the beginning of the year for all classes.
  • Saraswati Puja Discount: Get a 50% discount on admission fees during Saraswati Puja. A special offer to celebrate the festival of knowledge!
  • Mid-Term Admission: For classes other than 8, 9, and 10, admissions are open throughout the year. Join us anytime!
  • Special Admission: If another school restricts admission, we welcome students to join our school. This applies to classes other than 8, 9, and 10.

Our goal is to make education accessible and inclusive for everyone. If you have any questions about the admission process, feel free to contact us. We're here to help!
