Our School's Services

Welcome to Gyanpuja Boarding School! Explore our services below to learn more about our admission process, transportation, safety, and facilities. 🏫✨

Admission Process

We offer multiple admission processes to ensure every student has the opportunity to join our institution:

  • New Year Admission: Admissions open at the beginning of the year for all classes.
  • Saraswati Puja Discount: Get a 50% discount on admission fees during Saraswati Puja.
  • Mid-Term Admission: Admissions open throughout the year for classes other than 8, 9, and 10.
  • Special Admission: We welcome students restricted by other schools (excluding classes 8, 9, and 10).

Transportation and Safety

We prioritize the safety and convenience of our students with the following services:

  • 4 Buses: Safe and reliable transportation for students.
  • 1 Cafeteria: Provides healthy and nutritious meals.
  • 1 Guard House: Ensures the safety of the school premises.

Facilities

Our school is equipped with modern facilities to support learning and development:

  • Computer Lab: Equipped with 15 high-performance computers and the best Wi-Fi.
  • Playground: A fun and safe space with swings and slides.
  • Drinking Water: Clean and safe drinking water for all students and staff.
  • Toilets: Separate and well-maintained facilities for males and females.
